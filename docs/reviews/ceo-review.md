## Scope verdict (what to cut or change)

**Cut: nothing.** The PRD is large but internally coherent, and the user held scope. **Change**, in priority order:

1. **Consent must be captured at ingestion (P1, CRITICAL).** As written, no Sheets or Meta lead could ever become WhatsApp-eligible except by manual grant. Add consent field mapping with verbatim notice hash.
2. **One outbox, one claim, one eligibility function (C7, C8, A10).** Four reviewers designed the dispatch outbox four ways. Build `DispatchAttempt` once; the reaper turns lease-expired SENDING into AMBIGUOUS; operators resolve AMBIGUOUS. This is the #1 risk (duplicate or post-Stop sends).
3. **Stop is processed synchronously in the verified webhook transaction (C9).** The single exception to "no business logic in webhook routes".
4. **WhatsApp platform facts moved since the PRD was written** (landscape S2–S5): per-message pricing, portfolio-level messaging limits, India marketing frequency caps, and Meta's 2026 restriction on general-purpose AI assistants. AI replies must stay ancillary to the workspace's business service. Store raw provider values; hardcode no tiers or error semantics (§12.3).
5. **Money is BigInt paise** (C11) because PG `Int` caps at about ₹2.14 crore and property agencies are seeded.
6. **PDF via `@react-pdf/renderer` in a worker job** with one shared render model for HTML + PDF (C1). No Chromium dependency.
7. **Windows + OneDrive environment risk (risk #5).** Keep the SQLite DB, storage and backups outside OneDrive (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\auralead\`); spike the two-process claim race in Milestone 1.
8. **Truthfulness at breadth (risk #2).** About 30 screens. Use a `GatedAction` pattern so every unbuilt or unconfigured path is an explained restriction, and lint for empty handlers/TODO in `src/app/**`.
